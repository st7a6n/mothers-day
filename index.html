Dear Mom,

Growing up I always heard you talking about a "third power" in your parenting. This year, that power has manifested in all the wonderful journeys we have gone on, both around the world, and in our minds and souls. Through each city, we enter a new conversation with time, space, and the moments that give us meaning.

Of course, your love has always made me feel endless security, in all our years together. And yet, something I've been reflecting on recently is if there's also a "third kind of security" in your love that makes it so special. An intellectual safety, a mental harbour, a lighthouse for our thoughts. A security of always being understood, of knowing that one can be understood; that no matter what kind of absurdity one may throw out from the blackbox of consciousness, they will be understood. That is a kind of embrace warmer than any hug, a shield stronger than any else; and in that, you are the most fearless person I know.

Through you, I have learned how to love, and as I learn to embrace myself, I know that love can overflow, and go back towards you and many more in need. Thank you for making the world a less lonely place. Now as the greatest gift I can give, I can go forward and make this world less lonely for others too, just like you have always done for me, and countless other children of the world.

I love you, from here to eternity, to the end of space and time.

Sean Tan
